# Draft: lines for the easter eggs (not built; for Hunter's approval)

The places are built already and stay silent until these lines are approved. Each line opens once,
from the first companion in the party in the order given, and hands on to the next as the other
occasions do. Plain words, only what is in front of them.

## The twelfth name (remains by the bars of a gaol cell, now and then from depth 2)

Order: Aldric, Tobin, Mira.

- **Aldric**: "That's her. The girl. Elara. That's twelve."
  - hop to Mira: **Mira**: "You can stop counting now." **Aldric**: "No. I can't."
- **Tobin** (Aldric not with you): "I held the rope for her. She kept asking after her cousin."
  - hop to Mira: **Mira**: "Then she's done asking. Come away, Tobin."
- **Mira** (only her): "One of the twelve, I think. Somebody should know her name."

## Bram's shrine (the carving, in a treasury, once a run from depth 6 where the stone has it)

Order: Tobin, Mira, Aldric.

- **Tobin**: "That's it. That's the carving off Bram's shrine. The man with the crown. The folk kneeling."
  - hop to Mira: **Mira**: "The Guardian. They carved him up there and down here both."
- **Mira** (Tobin not with you): "The Guardian, and the dead kneeling to him. I've seen this in the Order's books."
  - hop to Aldric: **Aldric**: "Then it was down here first. Before any shrine."
- **Aldric** (only him): "A king, and people on their knees. Somebody wanted it seen."

## A perfect copy (now and then, on the catacombs' stone, one room is a room from above exactly)

Order: Tobin, Aldric, Mira.

- **Tobin**: "I know this room. It's up top, near where we came down. Same barrels. Same everything."
  - hop to Mira: **Mira**: "That isn't possible." **Tobin**: "I know it isn't."
- **Aldric** (Tobin not with you): "I've stood in this room. Up top. Every crate where it was."
- **Mira** (only her): "I've been in this room. Up top. Every piece just where it was. That can't be right."
