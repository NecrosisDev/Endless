# Draft: story beats past depth 3, and companions after a loss (not built)

Written 2026-10-22 for Hunter's approval before any of it goes into the roles file. Same rules as the
rest: plain words of the period, only what is in front of them, no readable texts down here, nothing
described before it is seen. The stage names are what the mod publishes (`endless_stage`), so each
line would open once, on the first arrival at that kind of stone.

## 1. Story beats by stage (one line each, the first present companion speaks)

**Sewers (stage 3, first reached at depth 4)**
- Aldric: "The twelve never came this far. I'd have found something of them. Whatever took them did it above us."
- Mira: "The Order's maps stop above this water. Nobody came back to draw the rest."
- Tobin: "Bram said the hole wanted feeding. Down here it smells like something's been fed."

**Mines (stage 5, first reached at depth 6)**
- Mira: "They dug for something here. My master's story said the ancients dug and found the Guardian. I think this is where the story starts."
- Aldric: "Picks and props and no miners. They dug until something stopped them."
- Tobin: "I was a digger's lad one summer. You don't leave tools lying like this. You run."

**Old brick (stage 6, first reached at depth 7)**
- Aldric: "Lower than any man has been and still the dead stand up. Something is holding them. I'd like to see what."
- Mira, before she has told you: "It's older here. I don't like how quiet it is."
- Mira, after she has told you: "It's close now. The thing that holds them. I can feel it, and I think it can feel me."
- Tobin: "I've had the feeling since the stairs and it hasn't gone. It's not one thing. It's the whole place."

**Past the seventh stage, when the stone repeats (stage 2 again)**
- Whoever is present, once: "The same stone again. Lower. It goes round, and it goes down."

## 2. A late-run beat that needs no new character: the proctor's seal

Aldric carries the proctor's seal (he says so when he admits what he did). Proposal: at the first
boss room of depth 9, after the fight, Aldric offers it to you. Three lines and two replies, once:

- Aldric: "The proctor's seal. I've carried it since I took it off him. I'd rather you had it. If one of us gets back up, it should go to Ardent."
  - You: "Keep it. You carried it this far." → Aldric: "Aye. I'll carry it, then."
  - You: "Give it here." → Aldric: "Here. Don't let the dead have it."

(The game has no item for it; it would be words only, unless you want a token item placed in his hands.)

## 3. Companions after a loss: two proposals

The mode has three companions and no more. Once one dies, the rest of the run has fewer voices.
Two ways to fill the gap, for you to choose or refuse:

**A. One more person from the story, found from depth 5 on, only after someone has died.**
Wat, a serving man from the Fallen Oak: one of the twelve, thrown in with the woman Tobin held the
rope for, who ran instead of fighting and has been hiding and stealing from the dead's packs. He is
a coward who knows it, which gives Aldric and Tobin something to say to him. Found in the farthest
room, like Tobin. First lines:
- Wat: "Don't. Don't, I've got nothing, I've got a knife and I can't use it. Are you from up top? Is there a way up?"
- You: "There is no way up. Come with us or stay here." → Wat: "With you. With you, then. I'll carry things. I can carry things."
- Aldric, if present, once: "The Fallen Oak. Then you knew the woman. Did you run when they took her?" → Wat: "I ran when they took me."

**B. Nobody replaces anyone.** The loss stays a loss, the mourning lines carry it, and the run gets
quieter. This is what is built now.

My recommendation is B for now and A only if you want a fourth voice; A costs a role, a record, a
set of occasion lines and hops with the other three, about the size of one companion's dialogue.

## 4. Lines for the area twists (mutators) and plans (archetypes), not built

The mod now publishes the area's plan and twist as flags, so one line each could open on arrival
(the first present companion). Proposed, plain, only what is in front of them:

**Dark** (the lamps are low): Aldric: "Half the lamps are out. Stay where I can see you." Mira: "Somebody let these lamps die. Or something did." Tobin: "Too dark. I don't like it. I'll go behind you this time."
**Ambush** (enemies near the door): Tobin: "Stop. They're close. Right by the door, I can feel it." Aldric: "Shields up from the door. They're waiting." Mira: "They're near. All of them, close. Go slowly."
**Silent** (the halls are empty): Mira: "Nothing. I can't feel one of them. That's worse." Tobin: "Where are they? There's always some. I don't like it." Aldric: "Empty halls. Whatever cleared them is still here. Keep together."
**Collapsed** (rubble everywhere): Aldric: "The roof's come down in places. Watch your footing." Tobin: "Half this is fallen in. Mind the stones." Mira: "It's falling in. Not today, I hope."
**Crypt** (rows of small rooms): Aldric: "Cells. Rows of them. Like a crypt." **Cistern** (one great hall): Mira: "One great hall. The water was here once." **Mine**: Tobin: "Long tunnels and nowhere to hide. Keep moving." **Halls**: Aldric: "Big rooms. Big rooms have big things in them."
